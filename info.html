About Census 2027

What is Census 2027?

Census 2027 is the sixteenth comprehensive national population and housing enumeration of India. It serves as the largest administrative and statistical exercise in the world, capturing the demographic, socio-economic, and cultural profile of over 1.4 billion citizens across every state, union territory, district, and village.

Why is it required?

The national census forms the bedrock for governance, policy formulation, and equitable resource allocation across India. It provides authentic, granular baseline data essential for planning public healthcare, education infrastructure, welfare schemes, transport networks, and urban development. Furthermore, census data determines constitutional representation through electoral delimitation and guarantees that government policies directly reach underserved communities.

What is the new Digital Enumeration process and how will it help?

Census 2027 marks a historic transition from traditional paper-based questionnaires to a 100% secure, paperless Digital Enumeration system. Citizens can now conveniently self-enumerate through a dedicated mobile-first web portal, while certified enumerators utilize encrypted digital tools for field data collection. This digital transformation eliminates manual data entry delays, ensures real-time validation, leverages geo-spatial boundary tagging to avoid duplication, and safeguards every citizen's personal data under rigorous national privacy and encryption standards.
