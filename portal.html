About ORGI

The Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner, India (ORGI) was established in 1961 under the Ministry of Home Affairs. It conducts the decennial census of India.

About Us

The Indian Census is the most credible source of information on Demography, Economic Activity, Literacy and Education, Housing & Household Amenities, Urbanisation, Fertility and Mortality, Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, Language, Religion, Migration, Disability and many other socio-cultural and demographic data since 1872. Census 2027 will be the 16th Indian Census.
